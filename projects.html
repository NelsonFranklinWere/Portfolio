Before I write code, I sit with the client and map the business: where money moves, where decisions get stuck, what the organisation looks like in three years. The system I build must fit that map — not force the business to adapt to software that was never designed for it.

That is why my work stays essential months and years after delivery. It was built around where the client was going, not just where they were.
