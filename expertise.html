Expertise of Nelson Franklin Were

I am Nelson Franklin Were — Director at Cres Dynamics Ltd, founder of OptioHire, and a systems builder who ships production infrastructure across ERP, finance, AI, and payments in Kenya.

Director, Cres Dynamics Ltd

As Director at Cres Dynamics, I lead delivery of custom business systems for organisations across cybersecurity, telecommunications, manufacturing, logistics, and retail. My role is not ceremonial — I am in the architecture, the client conversations, and the production deployments.

Cres Dynamics operates from Kivuli Towers, Westlands. We build ERP systems, finance platforms, AI automation, e-commerce, and full business operating systems. Under my direction, the company is pursuing its largest enterprise engagement to date: a 12-module BOS for Millenia Limited.

What I Specialise In

I specialise in systems businesses cannot operate without once they are running — integrated operational infrastructure, not websites that only look good.

Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)

Project lifecycle, team operations, approval workflows, audit trails, performance analytics. I have delivered three ERP systems for enterprise clients, including a platform serving 89 users with KES 2.3M in tracked project value.

Finance & Revenue Platforms

Institutional billing, subscriptions, invoicing, role-based financial dashboards, real-time balance updates. One platform I built tracks KES 4.97M across 127 users with 99.9% uptime.

Payments — M-Pesa & Beyond

M-Pesa Daraja API, Pesapal, IntaSend. I integrate Kenyan payment rails into production systems because most of my clients process real revenue in KES — not USD test transactions.

AI & Process Automation

Claude AI, Gemini, Langflow, n8n, custom ML pipelines. Three AI systems deployed. Fifty business processes automated. I use AI where it removes real operational friction — not where it makes a good demo.

Cybersecurity & EdTech Platforms

The OCH CybochEngine platform at cybochengine.africa — AI learner profiling, multi-track delivery, mentorship scoring, cohort management, certificates, and finance — is the deepest vertical system I have architected.

Cres Core — My Personal Methodology

Business first, technology second. Before architecture, I map how decisions are made, where money moves, where friction lives, and what the organisation will look like in three years. The result is systems that fit the business precisely.

I call this the Cres Core methodology. It is the reason systems I ship remain essential months and years after delivery. Clients do not outgrow the work because the work was built around where they were going.
